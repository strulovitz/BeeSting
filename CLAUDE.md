## 🚨 Rule #1 — THIS IS KLING, NOT HOLLYWOOD

**Every Kling clip is exactly ONE physical motion. Not two. Not "and then." Not a chain.**

Full rule and rationale: `KLING_NOT_HOLLYWOOD.md` — read it before writing any Kling prompt, any shot plan, any reference image description.

If you find yourself writing "and then," "followed by," "simultaneously," or chaining two verbs across two objects — STOP. You are about to waste Nir's credits and his patience.
