## Strategic principles (apply to all future videos)

### 1. No brand names in the title

Neither "BeeSting" (the video-project repo name) nor "Mad Honey" (the book) go in the YouTube title. Titles fight for **discovery** — the first 5 words must be keywords the audience is actually searching for (drones, drone swarm, jamming, electronic warfare, Ukraine, Operation Spiderweb). Putting a brand nobody has heard of in the title wastes the most valuable real estate on the internet.

Brands go in the **description** and the **pinned comment**, where the viewer has already clicked and is ready to convert.

### 2. "Mad Honey" vs "MadHoney" — both forms, different jobs

| Form | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Mad Honey** (two words) | Prose, sentences, spoken references | Natural English, reads like a book title |
| **MadHoney** (one word) | URLs, hashtags (#MadHoney), GitHub | Matches the repo, brand-friendly |

**⚠️ Critical:** Jodi Picoult's novel *Mad Honey* (2022) is a bestseller and an Oprah pick. Googling "Mad Honey" returns her book, not yours. **Always introduce Nir's book with the full subtitle — *Mad Honey: How Hierarchical AI Swarms Will Change Everything*** — because that unique phrase is what Google can index uniquely back to him.

### 3. Video first for discovery, book second for conversion

YouTube's discovery engine is roughly 1000× stronger than GitHub's. A Pentagon analyst will never stumble onto the book by Googling; they WILL stumble onto a YouTube video recommended to them. So:

- **Video = top of funnel.** Maximally searchable title.
- **Book = bottom of funnel.** Introduced under the fold, with full title, after the viewer has committed.
- **Pinned comment = second chance at the book.** YouTube ranks pinned comments highly.

Order of operations: Video wins the door. Book closes the deal.

## Design choices behind the description

- **First line = the $1,600 vs $32M hook** (from Chapter 7 of the book). YouTube only shows the first 2 lines before the "Show more" fold. That specific number stops the scroll.
- **Second paragraph = Operation Spiderweb.** News hook. Anyone searching "drone warfare" in 2025-2026 recognizes it.
- **"This is the warning you received before it happened"** — deliberate echo of the Prologue of the book ("The Warning") and of the outreach letter's phrase *"first formal written warning received by your organization."* Rewards readers of the book and the letter.
- **Closing equation at the bottom** — reinforces the same final text card the viewer just watched. The call to action is visible twice: on screen, then in the description.
- **Hashtags mix military and tech keywords:** Pentagon / DARPA / Ukraine pull military readers; #AIswarm / #distributedAI pull the tech crowd; #MadHoney starts seeding the brand hashtag for future videos.

---

## Do-not-do list

- ❌ Do not put "BeeSting" in the title.
- ❌ Do not put "Mad Honey" in the title.
- ❌ Do not describe the mechanisms (X/Y/Z/W) in the title — those are teaching devices inside the video, not hooks for a stranger.
- ❌ Do not use Hebrew or any non-English in the title — YouTube's algorithm penalizes mixed-language titles.
- ❌ Do not add hard-coded subtitles in Premiere — YouTube auto-generates captions, which can be edited in YouTube Studio in 10 minutes. Hard-coding eats hours and covers the Kurzgesagt visuals.
- ❌ Do not upload without the pinned comment ready — post it within the first 60 seconds of publishing.

## Template for future episodes

When Episode 2+ is made, reuse the structure:

1. **Title:** pure searchable content hook, no brand name.
2. **First 2 description lines:** the most visceral single fact from the episode (must work before the "Show more" fold).
3. **Identity line:** *"I am Nir Strulovitz. Independent inventor."* — keeps the author visible across the series.
4. **The body of the description:** the adapted outreach letter for that episode's theme (drones for Episode 1; naval for Episode 2 if it happens; etc).
5. **Book link block:** same formatting every time — three horizontal rules framing it.
6. **Closing equation:** same lines every time — *"AMERICA'S FATE EQUALS YOUR CALL. DO NOTHING — CHINA WINS. FORWARD THIS — AMERICA LIVES."*
7. **Pinned comment:** same template, same book link.
8. **Hashtag set:** keep #MadHoney in every video — this is how the cross-episode brand is built over time.

Consistency across episodes is how a channel becomes recognizable. The book is the anchor; each video is an episode of its mythology.
