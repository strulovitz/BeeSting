# Skunkworks / Innovation-Lab Outreach Letter

**Status:** WORK IN PROGRESS 2026-05-05. Recipients gathering started. Letter not yet assembled.

This letter targets a fundamentally different audience than the maker/hackathon letter:

- **Maker/hackathon ask:** *"Host a live demo where your audience IS the hive."*
- **Skunkworks ask:** *"Evaluate the architecture in your lab, run a benchmark on your hardware, internal-champion the result up your chain."*

Skunkworks-class organizations have engineers + hardware + procurement authority. They are closer to the original university letter's "stand up a few hundred real machines for one day" ask than to the maker letter's "audience IS the hive" ask.

The letter assembly for this category is **TBD** — likely closer to UNIVERSITY_LETTER.md than to MAKER_HACKATHON_LETTER.md, with role-words swapped to match (e.g. *your university* → *your lab* / *your innovation team* / *your evaluation group*).
