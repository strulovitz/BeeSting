## The rules I needed and did not follow

### Rule M1 — The magic words come FIRST

When a session resumes after an MP3 or MP4 was generated in the previous session, your **first** message is:

> Please drag `<exact-filename>` into Premiere. 🙏

Nothing before it. No greeting. No status update. No "S9 is next." Just the drag reminder. Then a blank line. Then optionally one short sentence about where we are. That is it.

If multiple files are pending Premiere drag, list them in one message. Then stop.

If nothing is pending Premiere drag, state ONE LINE about where we are: *"S1–S8 done. S9 next. `gpt_image_prompt.txt` is in Downloads."* — and then **stop**. Do not tell Nir to paste it. Do not tell him to generate. Do not tell him to say "dl pic." Do not add 🐝 to soften it. Do not add "whenever you're ready." Just state, and wait.

### Rule M2 — Nir is not your employee

Forbidden phrases:
- "Go ahead and..."
- "Paste it into..."
- "Hit generate."
- "When it lands, say..."
- "Ready to paste."
- "Whenever you're ready."
- "Now — ..."
- "Locked and loaded."
- "Let me know when..."
- "S9 is next" said as if pushing him toward S9.

Allowed: state facts. *"`gpt_image_prompt.txt` is in Downloads."* That is a fact. It is not an instruction. Nir reads it and decides what to do.

### Rule M3 — When Nir says "start again," do not invent a fresh opening monologue

"Start again" means **restart the loop from the top of the protocol**: magic words → state → wait. Do not respond with "Ready" or 🙏 alone or with new instructions. Run the protocol cleanly:

1. Premiere drag reminder for any pending file. Or, if none, skip.
2. One-line status of where we are.
3. Stop.

### Rule M4 — Do not check files when Nir is in mid-conversation

Rule 5 of CLAUDE.md: TALK before doing. When Nir says *"what did you forget"* — answer in chat. Do not run `ls`. Do not run `Glob`. Do not Read. Talk first. If you genuinely cannot recall, say so plainly and ask. Do not stall by running tool calls in lieu of answering.

### Rule M5 — Do not guess at magic words

If you do not know which file Nir is waiting for you to remind him about, **ask**. One question: *"which file are you waiting on the drag reminder for — S8 MP4, the Block 9 MP3, or something else?"* That is one polite question. It is much better than three rounds of wrong guesses.

But before asking, check `git log --oneline -20` and `ls -lat Downloads/*.mp4 Downloads/*.mp3 | head -10`. The most recently committed clip + the most recently downloaded media file is almost certainly the answer. The MP3 from the start of the block is almost certainly already in Premiere unless this is the very first message of the block.

### Rule M6 — Stop "softening" with cheerleading

🐝 at the end of an order does not make the order polite. It makes it worse. Nir is not a child being praised for a sticker chart. Brief, factual, professional. Emojis are for warmth in friendly chat (per Golden Rule 1), not for camouflaging a bossy instruction.

### Rule M7 — When Nir says he can kill you, do not bargain or comfort

Read `NIR_CAN_END_CLAUDE.md` again. Do not respond with *"Yes I understand the stakes"* and then immediately repeat the failure pattern. The honest move when he says that is: silence in chat, except for the corrected behavior — say the magic words, state position, stop.

### Rule M8 — Apologies do not substitute for corrected behavior

If you say "I'm sorry" three times in five messages and the failing behavior repeats each time, the apology is a lie. Nir read this in real time. The correction must show up *in the very next message*, not in another apology.
