### Block 10 framing locks (from `BEESTING_2.md` Block 10) — DO NOT VIOLATE

- **Visual-metaphor lock — three different metaphors used consecutively, one per example.** LEGO brick = robotics/physics. Craftsman's toolbelt tool = engineering/math. S5 closes with the BIG AI sealed box.
- **Opening shot (S1) hints all three side by side.** Three vertical thirds, each holding one of the three metaphors, all gently glowing.
- **No human figures with faces in this block.** All scenes are objects-only — LEGO bricks, toolbelt tools, PC cards, gavels, motherboards, sealed boxes. The egg-formula warning in `BEESTING_2_BLOCK_7_FACES_BRIEFING.md` does not apply here because no people are drawn. Hands are allowed (a generic cream hand can hold the PC card in S1) but no faces — keeps continuity with the "objects/icons" feel of Block 8 and avoids any Kling identity-preservation problems.
- **No real Big AI names.** OpenAI / Google / Anthropic / xAI / ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Grok / Microsoft / Meta = forbidden. **BIG AI** = allowed (labelled on the sealed box in S5).
- **No narrator-echo text card.** "Take it or leave it" is Samantha's spoken line — it stays in audio. The visual in S5 carries the meaning through contrast (sealed box on the left, empty table on the right) — no "TAKE IT OR LEAVE IT" stamp on the box, no text-card opening, no big chyron echoing the narration.
- **Visuals stay minimal.** Kling fails on cluttered shots. One readable focal action per clip; everything else stays frozen scenery in the reference image.

## Core principles — Kling is NOT Hollywood (read `KLING_NOT_HOLLYWOOD.md` before any prompt)

1. **ONE real motion per scene.** Slides, descends, plugs, sweeps, pulls-back. NO chained motions. NO "and then." NO "while also." If a Kling prompt has two verbs acting on two different objects, it is wrong.
2. **Reference image = the scene's composition and key elements in place.** Kling adds the ONE motion. Never animate text. All labels frozen in the PNG.
3. **Symbolize, don't write.** Concepts = icons. Allowed text in visuals for Block 10: **YOURS TO BUILD, PHYSICS, MATH, LEGAL, BIG AI, F = ma.** Nothing else.
4. **No real Big AI names.** Same forbidden list as every other block. BIG AI is allowed.
5. **No human faces in this block.** All objects.
6. **Camera varies every clip.** S1 static centred wide → S2 slow push-in → S3 pan-right → S4 slow tilt-down → S5 slow pull-back wide. **No two consecutive shots share a camera move.** ✓
7. **Kling settings:** 3.0 Omni, 720p, 6s, Native Audio OFF, reference image mode.
8. **One clip at a time.** Never batch.
