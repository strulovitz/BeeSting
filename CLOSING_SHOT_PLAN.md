# Closing Shot Plan — corrected to match what was actually built

**Original plan drafted:** 2026-04-19 (three separate closing shots: S75 aftermath, S76 corridor, S77 equations, S78 silent end card).
**Reality, rebuilt 2026-04-24:** only S75 (drone fires) was rendered as a separate clip. The S76, S77, S78 separation did not survive into the finished cut. Nir combined the equations and the YouTube/GitHub credits into **one single held closing shot** on near-black, with the Block 12 audio playing underneath. There is no separate silent end card. There is no Block 13 as a distinct final shot.

This file is now the corrected, canonical closing-plan spec for BeeSting Part 1, and the template for every future BeeSting episode's closing.

## The actual closing structure (canonical)

### Block 11 — Aftermath (Ep1 only — this is the missile-episode beat, not present in Ep2)
- **Narration (HOLY_TEXT.md Block 11):** *"Everyone inside was gone within four minutes."* + grave pause.
- **Visual:** photoreal empty-bunker corridor in dim red emergency lighting, drifting dust, no bodies (Hitchcock implication only). Matches the HUD-kill photoreal style from S07-S15.
- **Shot count:** S75 (drone fires, rendered) + a held corridor beat (inside S75 or a short follow-on, depending on edit).

### Block 12 — Combined closing shot (equations + credits, ONE block)

This is the combined final shot of the episode. It replaces the originally-planned S77 (equations) + S78 (silent end card) two-shot separation.

- **Narration (HOLY_TEXT.md Block 12):** *"America's fate equals your call. Do nothing — China wins. Forward this — America lives."* Spoken by Frank (Ep1) / Samantha (Ep2) over the shot.
- **Visual:** near-black background (very subtle dark-burgundy wash optional). Cream text, centered, stacked. The equations appear first in sync with the spoken narration, and by the end of the shot the YouTube/GitHub credits are also visible on the same screen — or the credits snap in under the last equation as the line lands. Either the text cross-fades within the single shot, or the credits sit below the equations from the start. The point is: one continuous held shot that ends with equations + credits on screen together.
- **Text content on the shot:**
  - **AMERICA'S FATE EQUALS YOUR CALL.**
  - **DO NOTHING — CHINA WINS.**
  - **FORWARD THIS — AMERICA LIVES.**
  - **YouTube: Nir Strulovitz**
  - **github.com/strulovitz**
- **Duration:** ~12-15s. Long enough for the spoken equations to land, plus a ~3-5s hold at the end where the credits are visible for the viewer to screenshot or click through.
- **Audio:** ElevenLabs narration of the equations, then silence for the held credit beat. Optional final music-note sting on the last beat.
- **End:** cut to pure black.

### Why this is not the banned narration-echo text reveal

The banned pattern is a mechanism-explainer shot whose content is the narrator's full sentence floating on screen as a redundant read-along. The closing block is different on two grounds:

1. **The display IS the point.** The equations are written in protest-sign / meme language, each fragment designed to be itself forwardable as a screenshot. The viewer is expected to pause, read, and share. The text is the artifact, not a decoration over the audio.
2. **The word "FORWARD" must appear literally** (per the original war brief) because the physical action asked of the viewer is pressing a forward/share button. Visual-verbal redundancy is the point, not an accident.

## For future episodes

Every BeeSting episode ends the same way: one combined closing shot, near-black, equations spoken + text + credits all on one screen together. Each episode may rewrite the equations to match its own themes (Ep2's equations will echo its AI-Big-Tech frame rather than Ep1's missile/warfare frame), but the structural pattern is fixed: **one combined closing block, not two.**
