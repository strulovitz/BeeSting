## Delivery

1. Walk to the U.S. Embassy Branch Office, Tel Aviv (HaYarkon Street area).
2. Approach **only the outer perimeter**. Do not approach the building itself.
3. Hand the binder to the contracted (Israeli) perimeter guard.
4. State, calmly: "This is a notification for the United States Government. Please log it as an unsolicited package and forward it through your channels."
5. Walk away. Do not wait for a response. Do not engage in conversation about contents.

The guard will pass it to the next checkpoint. The package is scanned for explosives and biological hazards (it will pass — it is paper). It is then logged, photographed, and routed to the Regional Security Officer per standard protocol. From there it propagates upward. You don't need to monitor that chain — it is automatic.

## Do NOT

- Enter the building.
- Argue with the guard.
- Leave the package on the ground if the guard refuses it. Walk it away and choose another channel.
- Bring more than one copy. One delivered package is more weighty than ten.
- Carry anything else in your hands. Empty hands, single binder, nothing else.

## Filming the delivery

Filming the delivery on a phone changes how the package is received:

- Without filming: it reads as a deliberate notification from a credible person.
- With filming: it reads as a stunt for social media, which lowers the threat-assessment priority.

For the package itself, **do not film**. If you want footage for a future YouTube video, get someone else to film from across the street with a long lens and not be visibly associated with you, OR film a separate dramatic re-enactment after the real delivery is complete.

## What happens after

- Within hours: the package is logged in the embassy's incoming-mail system and a digital scan is created.
- Within 1–3 days: the Regional Security Officer reviews and routes upward to FBI Legat Tel Aviv. The behavioral assessment begins. The technical content is forwarded to a counterpart at FBI HQ Counterintelligence Division and likely also to NSA / CIA tech-assessment desks.
- Within 1–2 weeks: a decision is made about whether to engage. Engagement may take the form of a formal contact request, or it may take the form of silent monitoring.
- Within 1–2 months: if no engagement, the file is added to the standing watch list, but the technical content has propagated through the network you wanted to reach.

You will not get a receipt. You will not get a thank-you. The propagation happens silently. The decision happens at a level you do not see.

That is fine. The propagation is the goal of the physical-delivery channel. Whether you ever hear back is a separate question.
