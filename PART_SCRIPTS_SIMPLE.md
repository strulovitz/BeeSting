## Part 1/14 — Drones (BUNKER VERSION — LOCKED 2026-04-13)

**Secretary (cold open, anchor delivery):**

BREAKING. A Chinese ballistic missile crossed the Pacific tonight — not built to explode, but built to carry. Inside it, a swarm of small cheap drones. The missile flew across the ocean, released the drones near their target, and then crashed empty into an American mountainside.

The drones flew straight into one of our most secure military bunkers. The bunker is buried deep under the mountain. Its walls are lined with thick metal, so no radio signal can reach in or out. No pilot from the outside could have flown those drones from a distance, even if they had wanted to. And yet — the drones came in through the air vents. They blew open locked doors. When our soldiers tried to block a hallway, another drone exploded through the ceiling and the rest dropped in behind it. Room by room, the swarm changed its plan whenever it hit a wall. It reached the generals sheltering at the bottom.

Until tonight, this was technically impossible. Every drone in history was either flown by a human on a radio, or too stupid to do more than fly straight and crash.

What we just watched is a new Israeli technology called KillerBee. Many small drones whispering to each other in a few words at a time, combining into one big thinking brain. It works inside a metal cage — because the drones talk to each other, not to a base. The Chinese military downloaded it off the internet last month. For free. The face of warfare just changed tonight.

**Manager (God paragraph, still in American news voice):**

Three weeks ago, America tried a drone attack of our own against China. We lost it in fifteen minutes. China did not shoot down our drones one by one. China did not jam our radios. China hit one single target with one hypersonic missile — the data center in California where the brain of our swarm lived. The building burned. Every drone we had in the air over the Pacific lost its mind in the same second. They froze. They drifted. They crashed. The whole attack stopped because one building stopped.

Tonight our soldiers inside the bunker tried to do the same thing to the Chinese swarm. They fought room by room. At one point they destroyed one queen drone — the drone that was running part of the swarm. They thought they had won. Nothing changed. Another queen was already flying in the swarm, carrying the same heavy brain on the same heavy hardware, doing the same job. Behind her, another. The swarm was built with backup queens from the start, because the small worker drones are too slow and too weak to ever take a queen's job — so the swarm brings extra queens along. From the outside, every drone looks identical. Our soldiers never knew which one was a queen and which was a foot soldier. And every queen they did destroy, another one was already flying behind her.

America has a heart. One missile on the heart, and the whole body dies. China has no heart. Many small brains in many small drones, and killing one changes nothing. In a real war, a side with one heart does not survive a side with no heart. America built the wrong shape.

## Part 2A/14 — DARPA

**Secretary (cold open):** A nice-looking recipe blog landed in her mother's WhatsApp. Her mother clicked it. That was enough. Every photo on her father's phone, every contact, every secret he ever told his wife about what he does for a living — all of it is now in the hands of the people who want to kill him. Her father is a scientist who builds weapons for the American military. His wife just opened the door for them, without ever knowing she did.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Your best engineers have families. Their wives, their kids, their parents, their old friends from college. Every one of those people is a door into your best engineer's life. A system running on a thousand cheap computers can knock on every door at the same time, forever. It does not sleep. It only needs one person to open one door. You have thousands of engineers. They have millions of doors.

## Part 2B/14 — Pentagon (The Matrix)

**Secretary (cold open):** An American general gave an order this morning that should have been impossible. He destroyed something no sane commander would ever destroy. He believed the satellite pictures. He believed the voice on the encrypted phone. He believed the orders coming down from above him. All of it was fake. It had been fake for months — built carefully for him, by a system designed to slowly break his judgment without him noticing. He did not know. His own aides did not know. By the time anyone realized, it was already done.

**Manager (God paragraph):** You do not need to fool the President. You only need to fool one man with a key. You show him fake pictures. You play him a fake voice of his commander. You feed him a fake version of the world for six months, until the fake world is the only one he sees. Then you give him the order. He turns the key. He is not insane. He is looking at a screen you painted for him.

## Part 2C/14 — DoD (The Building)

**Secretary (cold open):** The lights went out at the building in Texas where the American military keeps its brain. One small thing hit the cooling system. The computers overheated and burned themselves out in minutes. Everything the American military was about to do — every plan, every target, every weapon being guided — froze. The anchor cannot say where the war is now. She can say that there is no brain left to fight it with, and it will take more than a year to grow another one.

**Manager (God paragraph):** You put your entire military brain inside a handful of buildings that anyone can point to on a map. One missile. One bad janitor. One cut power line. One bad day — and you have no brain at all. China put its brain in millions of homes spread across the whole country. You cannot find them. You cannot bomb them. You cannot even count them. You built a giant wall. They built a swarm. The swarm flies around the wall.

## Part 3/14 — Humanoids

**Secretary (cold open):** A family was cooking dinner when three cheap robots walked up their driveway. The robots already knew who lived in the house. They knew the front door code. They knew the dog's name. They walked inside. The anchor does not describe what happened next. She says the robots cost less than a used car each, and there are already millions of them, and nobody knows who is sending them.

**Manager (God paragraph):** You spend millions to train one robot to walk. The Chinese train ten thousand robots at once, for free, on cheap home computers spread across their country. Their robot costs what your dinner costs. Yours costs what your house costs. Yours is smarter, one at a time. Theirs are many, and they learn together. Many cheap robots thinking together beat one expensive robot thinking alone. Ask anyone who has ever fought a swarm of bees.

## Part 4/14 — Intelligence (NSA)

**Secretary (cold open):** For the last year, a foreign system has been quietly reading every classified email the American government sends. It has been reading them from inside American computers, in American buildings, in American cities. There is no server to raid. There is no hacker to arrest. The messages are being read on the same machines the government typed them on — by software that nobody notices is running, because the software was quietly invited in months ago, by people who had no idea what they were installing.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Every secret you catch today, you catch because it passes through a wire you can touch. When people stop using your wire, you catch nothing. And the same software that blinds you turns the lights on for the other side — in every American kitchen, every American office, every American hospital. You go deaf and blind. They see and hear everything you used to. On the same day.

## Part 5/14 — Defense Contractors

**Secretary (cold open):** The biggest American weapons factory in her grandfather's town — the one that built the planes that won every war — announced today that it is closing. Her uncle was laid off last week. Her cousin was laid off this morning. The Chinese just won the next contract, and then the one after that, and then the one after that. The anchor does not say which contracts. She says the whole town will be out of work by Christmas, and that every other weapons town in America is next.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Your real advantage was never the factories. It was the engineers. Thousands of the best engineers in the world, thinking hard, solving problems slowly. Their real advantage is that they do not need engineers anymore. A thousand cheap computers, running free software, can try every possible design for a missile, or a plane, or a radar — all at the same time — and pick the one that works best. They do not need to be smart. They only need to be many. And they are many.

## Part 6/14 — Big Tech

Her friends have all switched to a different version, a Chinese one, that runs on their own computer and does not even need the internet to work. The biggest American technology companies announced layoffs this morning bigger than anything their industry has ever seen. The anchor says tens of thousands. By Friday.

**Manager (God paragraph):** You built an empire by charging people to use one giant computer of yours. The Chinese gave away software that turns a thousand home computers into the same thing, for free, forever. Nobody is going to pay you anymore when they can do it for nothing on computers they already own. Your subscribers leave. Your ads dry up. Your stock drops. Your investors walk. Your empire shrinks to the few customers who have not heard about the free thing yet. That number goes down every day.

Every deal that was supposed to be a secret is out. Every conversation that was supposed to be behind the curtain is on the front page. Bank CEOs are resigning by the hour. Your business runs on the conversations behind the curtain — the deals with the regulator, the favors traded between trading floors, the prices shared at the private dinner. Everyone always suspected. Nobody could ever prove it. A system running on cheap home computers can reach into every banker's private phone at the same time and pull the proof out. Not to steal the money. To remove the curtain. Your business cannot survive sunlight.

## Part 8A/14 — Big Pharma (Ozempic at $14)

**Secretary (cold open):** Her weight-loss pill costs a thousand dollars a month. She found out this morning that the same exact pill — the same chemical, not a copy — costs fourteen dollars a month in India. Forty different factories already make it there. She has been paying seventy times too much. Her husband too. Her sister too. Everyone she knows who takes it. She sits down at her kitchen table and starts counting how much money has been quietly taken from her over the years.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Your biggest product is a chemical that anyone can now make. China already makes it for the rest of the world. They will patent the next version of it in sixty countries before you even announce you were working on it. And the moment American patients find out what the real price is — which is happening right now, in every kitchen, and you cannot stop it — nobody pays your price anymore. The trillion-dollar future you promised your shareholders belongs to somebody else.

## Part 8B/14 — Big Pharma (The Cascade Killed Grandpa)

**Secretary (cold open):** Her grandfather was fine at fifty-five. Then a doctor put him on one pill. The pill caused a problem, so he was given a second pill for that problem. The second pill caused another problem, so he was given a third pill. By sixty-two he was on five pills. By sixty-seven he was confused and forgetful. At sixty-eight he broke his hip, because one of his pills had been quietly stealing calcium from his bones for years. At sixty-nine he died — demented, addicted to painkillers from the hip surgery, and not one doctor had ever told him that the very first pill had started it all. She only found out when her own home computer drew her a picture of the chain.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Your business is not one pill. Your business is the chain. One pill causes the next problem, which sells the next pill, which causes the next problem, which sells the next pill. Nobody invented this on purpose, but your whole industry quietly runs on it. The moment a computer on a patient's kitchen table draws the chain before the patient takes the first pill, the chain stops. Patients refuse the first pill. They never enter the chain. Your recurring revenue disappears — because nobody is walking into the machine anymore.

## Part 8C/14 — Big Pharma (McKinsey Priced Him)

**Secretary (cold open):** Her brother is dead from a pill a doctor gave him for back pain. She just found out the company that made the pill hired a famous consulting firm to sell more of it. The consulting firm wrote a real document — one you can actually read today — suggesting the drug company pay pharmacies a little money for every customer who overdosed. They put a price on a dead American, in writing. When everyone finally got caught, the consulting firm paid less per dead American than the price they themselves had written down. They made a profit on the settlement.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Right now your insurer pays a data company to quietly flag which of her relatives is costing too much to keep alive. When hospitals start running their AI on their own computers, that information stops leaving the hospital. The data company goes dark. The insurer loses its list. And every friend you have in the insurance business loses money the same week you do — because you were all making money off the same sick patients.

## Part 10/14 — Am I Bluffing?

**Secretary (cold open):** The anchor looks straight into the camera. She says the thirteen news reports you just watched on this channel were not predictions. They were warnings. Every single one of those things was built, tested, and posted on the internet by one man, in Israel, for free, years before any of this happened. His name is on the screen behind her. She says: the only reason the world is not on fire right now is that this one man would rather have a quiet life with his girlfriend than a loud one without her. And she says the world should probably find out what he wants before the quiet life stops being an option.

**Manager (God paragraph):** You already tried to silence him. You pushed his website to the bottom of Google. You banned his books from Amazon. You deleted his posts on Facebook. You denied him a visa to travel. Every one of those things worked perfectly — back when his work needed people's eyeballs to matter. But the thing he built now does not need eyeballs. It runs quietly, on other people's computers, in other people's countries, in languages you do not watch. Your old tools do not work on the new thing. There is exactly one tool left in your drawer that you have not used yet. It is the one you use to make a problem go away by paying it.
