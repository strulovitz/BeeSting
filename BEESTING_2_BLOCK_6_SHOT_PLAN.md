## Block at a glance

- **Named concept:** *Anonymization is Theater. Private Mode is the Cure.*
- **Visual style:** Kurzgesagt-style flat 2D motion graphics. Same flat-fill / clean-outline / no-gradient family as Blocks 3, 4, and 5 — but the mood here is **paranoid, surveilled, paper-trail**, like a 1970s spy thriller redrawn in flat 2D. Stillness in the camera, motion in the data, a feeling of being watched. Cool and unhurried, never frantic. The redemption shot at the end (S5) is the only warm beat in the block.
- **Palette:** **dark slate grey + red ink + cream**. Slate grey dominates backgrounds, walls, suits, building silhouettes — the colour of bureaucracy and surveillance offices. Red ink is for stamps, wiretap lines, surveillance tendrils, search-query text, data-trace pulses — the colour of exposure and bleeding-through. Cream is for documents, signage, banners, lamp light, and the warm windows of the Private Mode house in the closing shot. No gold (that's Blocks 3 and 5). No burgundy (that's Block 5). No alarm-orange. Just slate, red, cream.
- **Total runtime:** 5 clips × 6 seconds = 30 seconds, matched exactly to the 30-second narration. Audio-wins rule applies — clips can be trimmed 0–1 seconds shorter than 6s in Premiere if the narration line lands faster.
- **Argument arc of the block:** Snowden showed us the wiretap was real. Today it is automated and lawful — a court order rubber-stamped, every personal question you ever typed (cancer, a mistress, gay) exposed to anyone with the warrant. Big AI also sells your data labelled "anonymized," but a few details unmask you in seconds — anonymization is theater. The Hive's Private Mode ends both threats: your data never leaves your home. No court order can reach it. There is nothing to sell.

## Production rules — LOCKED, do not change without explicit Nir approval

These rules are paid-for-in-credits lessons from Blocks 3, 4, and 5. Respect them.

1. **Kling settings:** model = Kling 3.0 Omni, resolution = **720p** (never Pro — Pro is too expensive), Native Audio = **OFF** always, Duration = **6 seconds** per clip, mode = **reference image**.
2. **Never ask Kling to animate text.** Kling cannot write text, type text, reveal text letter-by-letter, or animate digits ticking. All labels, captions, banners, search queries, and stamps in this block must be **fully drawn into the reference image, frozen**. Kling's job is only to glow them, pulse them, or hold them still.
3. **Reference image must show the FINISHED state.** ChatGPT Image 1.5's job is to draw the final composition with all labels, characters, and stamps already in place. Kling's job is purely to add motion. Never ask ChatGPT Image to draw something "in the middle of happening" — just draw the moment of impact.
4. **Camera motion must vary every clip.** No two consecutive clips share a camera move. Camera grammar for this block is laid out in the Camera Variety Check section near the bottom — follow it.
5. **Full Kling prompts always.** Every Kling prompt must contain the four required elements per `KLING_PROMPT_LESSONS.md`: (1) visual style, (2) scene context, (3) animation action, (4) camera move. Lazy 2-line prompts are forbidden — Block 5 paid that price in static-looking clips. Each prompt below is already written in full; use them as-is or tighten only.
6. **Describe action in Kling prompts**, not just the static scene. Kling needs to know what moves, what flashes, what glows, what shatters. A static-sounding prompt produces a still-feeling clip.
7. **In Premiere:** trim each clip to match the exact narration line it covers. The audio is the master.
8. **Never name real Big AI companies.** Forbidden words anywhere: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft, Meta. Allowed: "Big AI", "Big Tech". For this block specifically, **"Snowden"** and **"NSA"** are allowed (they are named in the script and refer to a documented historical event), but in the visuals the surveillance agency is labelled **"THE AGENCY"** — not "NSA" — to keep the imagery generic and lawsuit-safe. The viewer's ear hears "NSA" in narration; the viewer's eye sees "THE AGENCY" on the building. That contrast is intentional.
9. **No real Edward Snowden likeness.** The hooded whistleblower in S1 is the *archetype* — a generic hooded figure at a laptop in a dim hotel room with TOP SECRET documents. No glasses, no specific facial features. The audience reads "Snowden" from the visual code (hoodie + hotel room + TOP SECRET papers + leaked surveillance map on screen). Do not attempt a likeness.
10. **One clip at a time.** Write ChatGPT Image prompt → wait → process PNG → write Kling prompt → wait → process MP4 → commit → drag into Premiere → only then start the next clip. Never batch.
11. **No title cards.** No clip opens with a "POINT FOUR" or "SNOWDEN DIAGNOSED" big-text card. Every clip opens with a *visual scene* — the laptop, the desk, the search bar, the folders, the house. Banners and captions inside the scene are fine; full-screen title cards are not.

## Continuity notes between clips

- **No recurring characters in this block.** The hooded figure in S1 is a one-shot; the watcher silhouettes in S3 are anonymous; the JOHN DOE portrait in S4 is generic. This block does not have to lock a character's clothing or hair colour across clips. That makes Kling's job easier here than in Block 5.
- **THE AGENCY (S1) and BIG AI (S4) are different buildings.** AGENCY is a tall dark monolithic government tower drawn on the laptop screen as a flat 2D map element. BIG AI is a tall dark slate grey corporate skyscraper drawn as a real-world building in the background of S4. They share the dark slate grey colour but have different shapes (AGENCY is monolithic and squat, BIG AI is tall and corporate). Do not visually conflate them.
- **The red APPROVED stamp (S2) and the red ANONYMIZED stamp (S4) share visual DNA** — both are red rings with cream block-capital lettering, drawn in the same Kurzgesagt style. This is intentional rhetorical rhyming: both stamps are state-and-corporate authorisations of intrusion. They don't need to be identical, but they should feel like cousins. The S5 REJECTED stamp also rhymes — same red ring shape — to close the loop visually.
- **The "PRIVATE MODE" sign in S5 is the only warm cream-glow text in the entire block.** All other cream text (TOP SECRET, COURT ORDER, EVERY QUESTION YOU EVER TYPED, ANONYMIZED, JOHN DOE, IDENTIFIED IN SECONDS) appears in cool unhighlighted form. PRIVATE MODE is the only text that glows from within. That's the visual cue for "this is the cure."

## Camera variety check

To keep the block from feeling sleepy, here is the planned camera grammar across all 5 clips. No two consecutive clips share a move:

1. **S1**: medium over-the-shoulder, slow push-in toward the laptop screen
2. **S2**: high overhead, static hold (stamp moves into frame from above)
3. **S3**: medium close-up, slow zoom-out / pull-back
4. **S4**: medium low side-angle, slow push-in toward the folder
5. **S5**: wide low-angle hero shot, slow upward tilt

Five distinct camera moves across five shots. Push-in (S1) → static (S2) → pull-back (S3) → push-in (S4) → tilt-up (S5). The push-ins frame the revelation moments (the leak, the unmasking). The static and pull-back frame the public-record moments (the stamp, the questions). The closing tilt-up lifts the audience's eye out of the surveillance and into the warm starry sky — the redemption beat of the block.
