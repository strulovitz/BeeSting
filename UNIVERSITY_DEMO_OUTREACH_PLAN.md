## Why this exists

Outreach to journalists (157 sent, ~7 YouTube views, zero engagement — see `WHY_NOBODY_REPLIED.md`) failed in part because there is **no live, real-hardware demonstration of the hive**. Everything Nir has built at home is technically valid but runs on virtual machines and a small home cluster — convincing as theory, not as event.

A university Computer Science department, with real machines and real students, can stand up a real-hardware hive in roughly **one day**. If they do it and publish results, the demonstration becomes the artifact that journalists, Big AI, and the Military–Industrial Complex have been waiting to pattern-match against.

The strategy is two-pronged, addressing two distinct audiences in parallel.

---

## Prong 1 — Top US AI universities

**Target:** Computer Science departments at the top US universities for AI research.

**The ask:** Run a one-day real-hardware experiment showing that, on **any task that can be parallelized**, the hive architecture beats Big AI on **both timing AND quality** — using real machines, not VMs.

**The bet:** If even one US department:
- Takes the threat to their country seriously
- Does not mentally suppress/repress the danger (the "this is too big, I'll pretend I didn't see it" reflex)
- Acts as loyal patriotic citizens who do not want the ruin of the USA

…then they can run the experiment quickly and pass the results **directly to** Silicon Valley Big Tech, the Military-Industrial Complex, the Pentagon, DARPA, NSA, DOD, the Army, etc. They have the standing to be heard, which Nir as an outside individual does not.

**Why the demo is small for them:**
- They have real GPU clusters, real student labor, real published-benchmark experience.
- The hive is open-source. They do not need Nir present.
- One day of compute. One paper-shaped writeup. Standard departmental work.
