## Block at a glance

- **Named concept:** *The Window-Maker Sends the Kid.*
- **Visual style:** Kurzgesagt-style flat 2D motion graphics. Same family of style as Block 3 (clean bold outlines, no gradients, flat fills, simple expressive characters).
- **Palette:** slate grey + red (Chinese flag accent) + cream + alarm-red explosion-bubble accents. Distinct from Block 3 (which was forest green + warm gold + cream). The mood here should feel cooler, more institutional, more dread-tinged than Block 3's warm sunny mechanism.
- **Total runtime:** 15 clips × 6 seconds = 90 seconds, matched to the 90-second narration. Audio-wins rule applies — clips can be trimmed shorter than 6s in Premiere if the narration line lands faster, and shots inside the closer can stretch slightly past 6s if needed.
- **Argument arc of the block:** A Big Tech executive needs insurance against AI/internet failure. Real insurance is too expensive. China shows up and offers the same protection for free. Then it turns out China is also the one creating the very disruptions the insurance protects against — like an arsonist running a fire-extinguisher business. Every disruption pushes more enterprises onto the Chinese Hive, shrinking American Big AI bit by bit until it's too late. The closer reframes this as a known historical playbook: the US did the same thing to Russia by way of Ukraine, and now China is running the AI version of the same scam.

## Production rules — LOCKED, do not change without explicit Nir approval

These rules are the lessons from earlier sessions today. Every one of them has been paid for in wasted Kling credits. Respect them.

1. **Kling settings:** model = Kling 3.0 Omni, resolution = **720p** (never Pro — Pro is too expensive), Native Audio = **OFF** always, Duration = **6 seconds** per clip, mode = **reference image** (not start/end frame, unless the plan specifically calls for chaining two frames).
2. **Never ask Kling to animate text.** Kling cannot write text, carve text, type text, or animate digits ticking up/down. Anything that requires the content of text to change over time will fail and waste credits. If the plan needs a label, that label must already be fully drawn in the reference image, and Kling's job is only to flash, glow, or hold the label still — never to write or change it.
3. **No graphs, charts, or numeric counters** that have to update in the animation. Kling renders these poorly and inconsistently. Replace any "counter ticking down" idea with something physical — coins draining, bills floating away, a meter needle dropping, a pile shrinking.
4. **Reference image must show the FINISHED state.** ChatGPT Image 1.5's job is to draw the final composition with all labels, signs, captions, and characters already in their finished positions. Kling's job is purely to add motion to that frozen frame. Never ask ChatGPT Image to draw something "half-done" — that confuses both ChatGPT Image and Kling.
5. **Camera motion must vary every clip.** No two consecutive clips with the same camera move. Mix wide holds, slow pushes, slow pulls, side pans, dramatic angles, overhead views, etc. Variety keeps the block from feeling sleepy.
6. **Describe action in Kling prompts**, not just the static scene. Kling needs to know what is happening — what moves, what flashes, what falls, what migrates. A static-sounding prompt produces a still-feeling clip.
7. **In Premiere:** trim each clip to match the exact narration line it covers. Do not lock all clips to exactly 6 seconds. Some clips will land at 4–5s in the final cut, others closer to 7s. The audio is the master.
8. **Never name real Big AI companies.** Forbidden words anywhere in any prompt, caption, or script: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft, Meta. Allowed: "Big AI", "Big Tech", "American cloud giants", "the four", and direct names of Chinese players (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, Alibaba, Moonshot) when relevant.
9. **Sacred line:** "your data never leaves the building" — never paraphrase this if it appears.
10. **One clip at a time.** Write ChatGPT Image prompt → wait → process PNG → write Kling prompt → wait → process MP4 → commit → drag into Premiere → only then start the next clip. Never batch.

### S1a — Internet snaps, Big AI goes dark

**Narration line:** *"Point two. No internet needed. Every company depends on the internet. When the internet breaks, Big AI breaks."*

**Reference image:** A wide shot of a small flat 2D corporate cityscape under a slate-grey overcast sky. Three or four boxy office buildings line the frame, each with several lit windows. The cable is shown in the act of being snapped — a clean break in the middle of the frame, with jagged sparks exploding out in alarm-red. Inside every visible office window, the computer screens already show the words "BIG AI OFFLINE" in cream text on an alarm-red background. The whole image should communicate "the lifeline just got cut and the city went into panic mode." Clean bold outlines, flat fills, no gradients.

**Palette:** slate grey buildings, alarm-red sparks and screens, black cable, cream text.

**Animation:** The sparks at the cable break flicker and shoot outward. The "BIG AI OFFLINE" screens pulse and flash in alarm-red. Lights in some office windows flicker on and off. Camera slowly pushes in toward the snapped cable as the panic ripples outward. No text appears or changes — all text is already on the screens.

**Camera:** slow push-in toward the cable break.

### S7a — The Ukraine playbook

**Narration line:** *"This is not new. The United States pushed Ukraine into war with Russia so NATO would buy American weapons. Europe paid. Americans profited."*

**Reference image:** A wide top-down map of the North Atlantic region in three horizontal sections from left to right. **LEFT (USA):** The United States as a cream-coloured landmass on the left of the frame, with a large American flag planted on it. Above the USA, a giant glowing green dollar sign "$". On top of the USA, big stacks of green dollar bills piled up — the USA looks prosperous, rich, satisfied. **MIDDLE (Atlantic Ocean):** Deep navy-blue ocean. Two parallel streams cross the ocean simultaneously in clearly opposite directions, with directional arrows so the direction is unmistakable: weapons (rifles, tanks, missiles) flying LEFT-TO-RIGHT eastward from USA toward Ukraine; dollar bills flying RIGHT-TO-LEFT westward from frightened NATO countries back to USA. **RIGHT (Western Europe / NATO):** Cream-coloured Western European landmass with visible borders. Frightened cartoon faces drawn on France, Germany, the United Kingdom, and Poland — wide scared eyes, sweat beads, mouths in worried "O" shapes. The small label "NATO" written in cream block letters across these countries. These rich Western European countries are the source of the westward money stream — they are paying. **FAR RIGHT (Ukraine):** On the easternmost edge of Europe, Ukraine drawn in alarm-red and completely engulfed in large flame icons — Ukraine is BURNING, totally devastated. NO money flows out of Ukraine. Ukraine is the victim, sacrificed for the racket. **EAST OF UKRAINE (Russia):** Russia drawn as a large dark slate-grey landmass with a stern threatening cartoon face — the bogeyman whose threat scared NATO into buying American weapons. **BANNER:** Stamped across the top of the frame in big bold cream block letters on an alarm-red banner: "AMERICA'S RACKET" (deliberate callback to the BROKEN-WINDOW RACKET banner from S5b — same scam, historical version).

**Palette:** cream USA and Western Europe, deep navy Atlantic, alarm-red burning Ukraine and banner, dark slate looming Russia, red American flag, green dollar bills and dollar sign, frightened pale European faces.

**Animation:** Flames flicker and dance across burning Ukraine. The weapons stream flows eastward steadily. The money stream flows westward steadily. The pile of bills on the USA grows visibly with each passing dollar. The giant "$" above the USA glows and pulses warmly. The frightened European faces tremble slightly. Russia's threatening face stares east unmoving.

**Camera:** wide overhead map view, holds steady so the entire three-section geography is legible.

**Why this composition (locked 2026-04-27, Nir):** The earlier draft of this scene framed the weapons as flowing USA→Europe and the money as flowing Europe→USA, which mirrored the official Western narrative. Nir corrected this: the actual reality being communicated is that *Ukraine is the sacrifice, not the customer*. America does not care about Ukraine — Ukraine just burns. The point of pushing Ukraine into war was to scare the *rich* NATO countries (France, Germany, UK, Poland) into buying expensive American weapons. The money flow is therefore Rich-NATO→USA, while Ukraine merely burns and gets nothing. This composition is the visual twin of S7b's China-Pacific-USA grammar (aggressor on left with flag + $, victim on right burning, product flows L→R, money flows R→L) — that twin is the punchline of the whole block.
