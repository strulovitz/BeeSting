# NO FULL-SENTENCE TITLE CARDS OR TEXT OVERLAYS IN BEESTING

**Every shot is 6 seconds of moving video. Never a still image. Doors swing shut, chisels carve, dust flies, stakes drive in, coins drop, pipes snap, hands hammer. Motion in every frame.**

## The actual rule, not my earlier confused versions of it

**Short labels are fine and used constantly.** 1-to-3-word tags, stamps, signs, icons, inline captions, HUD callouts — these are everywhere in BeeSting Part 1 and they work great. Examples already locked in the repo:

- *"Kamikaze Mode: ON"* — HUD text in Part 1 S09
- *"RECONNECTING..."* — phone-screen label in Mechanism X S17
- *"DECOY"* — coral label on a fake submarine in Mechanism W S73a
- *"$ BILLIONS"* — floating label above the datacenter in Mechanism Z S56
- *"< DINNER"* — inline label next to a drone in Mechanism Z S59
- *"IN THE DIFFERENCES"* — landing label in Mechanism W S74b
- *"NumPy / SciPy / FilterPy"* — subtle stack label in Mechanism W S70b
- *"WORKER" / "MANAGER" / "PLATFORM"* — rung labels on the three-rung ladder
- *"NSA · ADVERTISERS · INSURERS · EMPLOYERS"* — labels on the four pipes in Block 3 S5
- *"PATIENT RECORDS" / "TRADE SECRETS" / "CLASSIFIED DESIGNS"* — labels on safe-deposit boxes
- *"MELANOMA-SPECIALIST.MODEL"* — file label on a disk icon

All fine. All short. All integrated with an object or action. Kling renders 1-3 word labels reliably.

**Full-sentence title cards and full-sentence text overlays are forbidden.** The forbidden pattern is a shot whose main content is an entire sentence from the narration, floated on screen as if the viewer needs to read along. This is the silent-film-era move BeeSting does not do.

Concrete forbidden examples (these were in my draft of Block 3, 4, 5 and are being replaced):

- ❌ *"Title card: 'Point 1. Nothing leaves your building.' Brass vault-door icon on charcoal background."*
- ❌ *"Title card: 'Point 2. A free source of income for Joe Sixpack and Plain Jane.' Gold coin icon on forest-green background."*
- ❌ *"Title card: 'Point 3. Train once, copy forever.' Ghost-radiologist icon on silver-violet background."*
- ❌ *"Text card overlay: 'No bank. No box. No pinky-swear.'"*
- ❌ *"Text card: 'Not a movement. A state-backed campaign.'"*
- ❌ *"Text card overlay: 'The HIVE will do to Big Tech!'"*
- ❌ *"Text card: 'The stake has already been driven.'"*

Why these are forbidden:
1. **Samantha is already speaking those exact words.** Showing them on screen at the same time is redundant.
2. **Kling butchers full-sentence rendering** — letters swim, words wrap wrong, the reader spends the whole 6 seconds trying to parse a slide instead of watching the story.
3. **The pattern the existing BeeSting Part 1 shot plans use is different:** a simple concrete visual metaphor of what the narration is saying. For "Nothing leaves your building" → a building + a sheet of paper trying to fly out + a big red STOP sign blocking it. For "No bank, no box, no pinky-swear" → three quick tiles, each with an object (bank / box / pinky-promise hands) and a big red X crossing it out. The narration delivers the words; the visual illustrates the concept.

## The decision rule for any shot

When writing a shot plan, ask two questions:

1. **Is the text on screen 1–3 words?** If yes, fine, go ahead — label it on an object or HUD, make sure Kling can render it.
2. **Is the text on screen a full sentence or most of a narration line?** If yes, cut it. Keep the full sentence in Samantha's spoken narration only. Replace the visual with a simple concrete illustration of what the narration is saying — same style as the existing BeeSting Part 1 mechanism shot plans — using objects, icons, X signs, STOP signs, arrows, whatever carries the concept without words.

Chyrons behind a live on-screen anchor (Samantha's 9-point news-desk summary cards in Block 2.8) are their own category and already approved by Nir — they are broadcast-news chyrons, not title slides.

## Scope

Applies to BeeSting Part 1, Part 2, and every future episode. If an old shot plan contains a forbidden full-sentence title card or text overlay, upgrade it to a simple visual.
