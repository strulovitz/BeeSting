# NO BORING TEXT-ON-COLOR TITLE CARDS IN BEESTING VIDEOS

**The rule:** punchy section-title or punchline text IS allowed in BeeSting — but it must live inside a real animated visual scene, not on a flat colored background with nothing else happening. The forbidden thing is silent-film-era boredom, not the words themselves.

**Why:** this is 2026, not 1926. BeeSting is not a silent film. A shot whose only content is a few words centered on a solid color is a 6-second dead zone — it tells the viewer "the animator ran out of ideas" and it tells the narrator "repeat the words I just said, slower." Every shot must earn its runtime with real visual content. But that does not mean text is banned — it means text must be inside a scene, not the whole scene.

## What IS forbidden

- A shot whose visual is just text on a solid color: *"Title card: 'Point 3. Train once, copy forever.' Icon on silver-violet background."*
- A shot whose only content is a text-card overlay of the narration punchline appearing over an otherwise-empty frame: *"Text card: 'The stake has already been driven.'"*
- Any shot description that reads like a slide from a PowerPoint deck.

## What IS allowed (and often great)

- **Text integrated into a working visual scene.** The words *"Point 1. Nothing leaves your building"* carved into the face of a brass bank vault door while the door swings shut. The words *"The HIVE will do to Big Tech"* chiseled into a tombstone by an invisible hand. The words *"NYC HOSPITALS CANCEL PALANTIR"* printed on a newspaper front page that lands on a desk with a thud. These are visual scenes that happen to contain text, not text slides.
- **Diegetic labels on objects inside the scene.** A pipe labeled **NSA · ADVERTISERS · INSURERS · EMPLOYERS**. A safe-deposit box labeled **PATIENT RECORDS**. A file labeled **MELANOMA-SPECIALIST.MODEL**. The label is on the object, not on a slide.
- **News broadcast chyrons** behind a live on-screen anchor. Samantha's 9-point summary in Block 2.8 uses a single static **CATCHY-NAME TEXT CARD** near her talking head. This is broadcast-news discipline — the anchor is on screen, the text is a chyron, not a slide. Approved by Nir on 2026-04-22.
- **Newspaper / book / magazine covers appearing in frame** with their own headlines. The headline is on the object, not on a title card.
- **Punchy section-title words emerging from animation.** If the moment wants to deliver the title of the section with impact, build that as an animated reveal integrated into a visual scene — the letters can fly in, assemble from sparks, be painted by a hand, appear on a protest sign held up in a crowd, etc. The word "Point 3" can absolutely appear on screen — it just cannot be alone on a colored rectangle with a tiny icon.

## How to upgrade a forbidden title card into a real visual

When a draft shot plan contains *"Title card: ..."* or *"Text card: ..."* with nothing else happening, upgrade it:

| Forbidden (flat text-on-color) | Allowed (same words inside a real scene) |
|---|---|
| *"Title card: Point 1. Nothing leaves your building. Brass vault-door icon on charcoal background."* | *"Close-up of a brass bank vault door swinging shut. The words POINT 1 — NOTHING LEAVES YOUR BUILDING are engraved into the face of the vault door itself, catching the dim vault light as the door closes."* |
| *"Text card: The HIVE will do to Big Tech!"* | *"A chisel-in-hand is carving the words THE HIVE WILL DO TO BIG TECH into the third tombstone in a graveyard at dusk, stone dust flying."* |
| *"Text card: The stake has already been driven."* | *"A wooden stake stands upright in the foreground ground, driven in; its shadow stretches across a landscape of glowing hospital buildings on the horizon. The words THE STAKE HAS ALREADY BEEN DRIVEN are burned into the stake itself with a small plume of smoke still rising."* |

Same words. Completely different energy.

## Scope

This rule applies to BeeSting Part 1, Part 2, and every future episode. If a shot plan contains a flat-color title or text card, upgrade it before the block ships.
