# Where Silicon Valley Gets News — outreach roadmap

**Source:** Google AI Overview answer to "where do Silicon Valley get news" (early 2026 snapshot, captured 2026-05-01).

**Purpose:** Track which Silicon Valley news sources we have already covered in `ai_tech_journalists_list.md` and which still need research.

## Primary Tech & Business News Sites

- ✅ **The Information** — widely considered the most influential source for tech execs and VCs. Deep, exclusive reporting. (rows 110–123 in list)
- ✅ **Techmeme** — highly curated automated aggregator, default morning site for breaking tech headlines. (rows 124–128 in list)
- ✅ **TechCrunch** — standard for startup news, funding rounds, VC activity. (rows 129–157 in list)
- ❌ **Wired** — covers cultural and social impact of technology
- ❌ **Axios Tech** — concise, high-level summaries and analysis
- ❌ **SiliconValley.com** — tech policy, local startups, business news
- ❌ **The Verge** — convergence of tech, culture, science
- (n/a) **Hacker News** — Y Combinator aggregator, community-driven; no individual journalists to email

## Specialized & Independent Sources

- (n/a) **Hacker News** — see above
- ❌ **The San Francisco Standard** — local tech, policy, AI industry trends in SF
- ❌ **Business Insider / SAI** — business and tech industry updates

## The Rising "Tech-Owned" Media Ecosystem

(Silicon Valley building its own media to bypass mainstream reporting)

- (n/a) **X (formerly Twitter)** — real-time debate platform, not an outreach target via email
- ❌ **All-In Podcast** — Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis, David Sacks, David Friedberg
- ❌ **MTS (Monitoring The Situation) / The Peak** — 24/7 livestream and news show on X, "pro-tech" alternative to cable news
- ❌ **TBPN (The Business and Technology News Network)** — recently purchased by OpenAI
- ❌ **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) "Future"** — a16z's proprietary media property, pushing pro-tech agenda
