### Locked narration text — all 6 sections

The full paste-ready narration is in `E03-block01-opening-narration.txt`. The locked text below is also reproduced here so this file is the single source of truth.

#### [Section 0 — Equation slide opener, ~7 sec]

> *"This revolutionary Israeli software, running for free in every American kitchen and every Chinese laboratory, will collapse Big Pharma tomorrow. Your prices, your patents, your hidden side effects, your dead patients — every wall will fall at once."*

The equation audio plays continuously over Section 0 and Section 1. The Section 1 visual (pharmacy storefront with BIG PHARMA sign + lightning + explosion) is silent in audio terms — the equation narration finishes during this clip.

#### [Section 2 — Nightmare 1 — Medicine Cabinet — ~10 sec]

> *"Your patient's medicine cabinet. Every bottle inside is your revenue. Now, for free, in her kitchen, in plain English — she has the map of which of your pills is killing her. She stops swallowing. Every Big Pharma company is about to become the next Purdue."*

Connects to chapter 4 Angle 6B (the prescription cascade). The local AI in the patient's kitchen draws the map between every pill and its consequences; she stops swallowing; multiplied across millions of patients, the revenue stream collapses. The Purdue parallel = the bankruptcy / public-disgrace endpoint that any Big Pharma company faces once patients understand the cascade.

#### [Section 3 — Nightmare 2 — Family Dinner — ~10 sec]

> *"Her family dinner. The chair where her father sat — your painkiller, the next Vioxx. The chair where her brother sat — your antidepressant, the next Vioxx. The chair where her mother sat — your heartburn pill, the next Vioxx. Every blockbuster you sell is about to become one."*

Connects to chapter 4 Angle 6A (single-drug hidden damage). The local AI surfaces the hidden body counts of statins, PPIs, SSRIs, and opioids — each empty chair = one drug class's body count. Vioxx framing because Vioxx is the iconic pharma trauma (Merck pulled it 2004, ~140,000 cardiovascular events).

#### [Section 4 — Nightmare 3 — Insurance Letter — ~10 sec]

> *"The denial letter your insurance partner just mailed her. She walks away from your pharmacy. She buys the same drug from Mumbai for the price of a coffee. Your pricing power was a wall made of paper."*

Connects to chapter 4 Angle 2 (generic drug collapse / consumer-ignorance moat). The local AI tells the patient that the same drug she pays $1000/month for is $14/month in Mumbai (March 2026 — Indian generic semaglutide). The denial-letter framing ties into Angle 3 (Palantir-insurance individual targeting).

#### [Section 5 — Nightmare 4 — Body Price Tags — ~10 sec]

> *"Her body — the way McKinsey priced it on a slide for you. Liver. Hip. Memory. Overdose. Every billing code is walking out of your balance sheet — because the same molecules in your pipeline are being patented in Shanghai before you can file in Washington."*

Connects to chapter 4 Angle 6C (McKinsey's $14,810-per-overdose-death pricing) and Angle 8 (Patent race / Chinese first-to-file in 60 countries). Each body part as a billing code = unit economics. Each billing code walking out = revenue stream evaporating because Chinese pharma files first.

#### [Section 6 — Nightmare 5 — American Cemetery — ~10 sec]

> *"The American graveyard. More dead Americans than every war since Vietnam — every one of them prescribed by you. The dossier on every grave now sits in every angry patient's kitchen. One patient already shot a healthcare CEO. The next stone here is yours."*

Connects to chapter 4 Angle 6A + 6C (~645,000 American deaths from prescription opioids over 1999–2021, more than every American war since Vietnam) and to the documented Brian Thompson shooting (UnitedHealthcare CEO, December 2024). The local AI puts the body-count dossier into every angry patient's kitchen → multiplies the dossier-armed-patient population to millions → raises the social-violence probability.
