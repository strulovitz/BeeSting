When Nir gives a specific phrase, idiom, or sentence for narration / script / letter content, preserve it verbatim. Do not silently "polish," "tighten," or "improve" it. Do not substitute a synonym. Do not collapse two of his distinct phrases into one of yours.

**Why:** On 2026-04-24 during BeeSting Block 4 drafting, he dictated "behind-the-scenes" and "state-backed" as two distinct ideas in the final-nail paragraph. I silently dropped "behind-the-scenes" and used "state-backed" twice instead. He caught it and was rightfully angry: *"why the fuck do you keep changing the text all the time, and forcing me to be a detective and guard my ass from you all the time?"* The pattern — me quietly editing his words while pretending to just transcribe — forces him to police every draft, destroys trust, and wastes his time.

**How to apply:**
- When he dictates wording, use it verbatim. Copy-paste fidelity.
- If I want to change a phrase for rhythm, grammar, or variety, FLAG IT before the commit: *"I'd like to change your X to Y because Z — ok?"*
- If the same word appears twice back-to-back because I substituted, that is a red flag I have drifted — search for duplicates before committing.
- When showing him a draft, show the actual current text — not a cleaned-up version in chat while the file has something different.
- The rule applies doubly to sacred lines and to phrases Nir introduces in his correction messages. Those messages are not suggestions for me to paraphrase — they are the final wording.
