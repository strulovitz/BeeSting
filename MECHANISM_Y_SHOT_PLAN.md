**Locked:** 2026-04-19 (rewritten after Nir called out the first draft for being diagram-soup with lazy text-card bookends)

**Audio file:** `audio/E01-block08-mechanism-y.mp3` (Frank voice, ElevenLabs)
**Visual style:** Kurzgesagt-style flat 2D motion graphics, duotone palette (**deep burgundy/wine background + mustard gold highlight + cream accents**), per `MECHANISM_SECTION_VISUAL_STYLE.md`.

Total runtime: 15 × 6s = 90s. Matches Block 8 narration (89s) with ~1s slack.

## Story arc — one recurring villain, one recurring swarm

The segment follows a single antagonist: **THE JAMMER** — a sweaty cartoon Kurzgesagt-style villain in a military general's cap, driving a comically oversized jammer truck with a big dish antenna. He is the SAME character in every shot where an adversary appears. He starts cocky (S32), becomes the screaming ear-jammer in the metaphor (S34), defeats the old-architecture puppet drone (S37), then LOSES four times in a row against the hive (S40, S41, S42, S43), panics at the scale in S45, and is finally defeated + winked-at by a little drone in S46.

The hive side has recurring characters too: the **queen drone** (with a stylized crown icon floating over her body as a tag, NOT insect anatomy) and her **worker buddies** who text each other happily through every obstacle.

This gives the viewer one protagonist to root for, one antagonist to boo at, and a visual through-line. **No title cards. No giant text reveals. Punchlines are character moments.**

## Character cast (consistent across every shot they appear in)

- **THE JAMMER** — sweaty Kurzgesagt-style cartoon villain, round body, military general's cap with a small cream star on the front, permanent scowl, gets redder and sweatier as the segment proceeds. Always wearing the same cap. Drives an oversized cream-outlined military jammer truck with a big dish antenna on its roof. First appears S32. Recurring in S34 (as the ear-screamer in the metaphor), S37, S40, S41, S42, S43 (tiny in corner), S45, S46.
- **FRIEND** (street metaphor) — round head with round cream glasses, cups hands around mouth, always yelling. Only in S33, S35.
- **LISTENER** (street metaphor) — round geometric head, always cupping hand to ear or clamping hands over ears. Only in S33, S34, S35.
- **OLD MILITARY DRONE** — a larger, more-elaborate mechanical quadcopter with extra antennae and a serious-looking eye-icon; distinct from the hive drones by size and design complexity. Tethered to a distant datacenter via a comically long cream puppet-string. Only in S36, S37.
- **QUEEN DRONE** — mechanical quadcopter with a small stylized crown icon floating ABOVE her body as a symbolic tag (NOT insect anatomy). Only in S38, S39, S40 (aerial view), S41.
- **WORKER DRONES** — mechanical quadcopters without a crown, smaller than the queen, friendly expressions (small cream eye-icons). Several. S38, S40, S41 (new arrival), S42, S43, S45 (many).
- **KIDS** (schoolroom metaphor) — simple cream cartoon students at desks. Only in S44.

Use these descriptions verbatim in reference-image prompts when generating so the characters are visually consistent across clips.
