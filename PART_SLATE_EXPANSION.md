# Part Slate Expansion — From 10 to 14 Parts

**Decision date: 2026-04-13**

This document records a deliberate expansion of the locked Part Slate from 10 parts to 14 parts. The expansion was driven by reading every chapter of MadHoney in full and discovering that two chapters contain multiple genuinely distinct audience fears that cannot be collapsed into a single video without losing the strongest beats.

**This document supersedes the "Part Slate (LOCKED ORDER)" section of SERIES_BIBLE.md for the part count only.** All other rules in the bible — the three-act structure, the two hard conversions, the angels-before-god rule, the never-technical rule, the `1 MONTH FROM NOW` chyron, the title format — remain in effect unchanged. Only the number of parts and the titles/order change.

---

## Why the slate expands

Going through the chapters chapter-by-chapter revealed three patterns:

1. **Most chapters contain one shared fear** across their named target companies or agencies. The named entities in the chapter title are interchangeable — a single God paragraph fits all of them. These stay at one video.

2. **Chapter 2 (Pentagon) contains three structurally distinct fears**, one for each of DARPA, Pentagon, and DoD. The chapter itself is written in three separate sections, each with its own attack vector, its own villain mechanism, and its own God paragraph. Collapsing them into one video loses two of the three strongest beats in the book.

3. **Chapter 4 (Pharma) contains ten explicit angles**, but the angel-forwardable secretary beats and the manager-terrifying boardroom beats do not line up one-to-one. The right structure is to bundle each video as (one secretary fear) + (one DIFFERENT manager fear), choosing the three scariest of each group and pairing them where the link is natural.

## Chapter 2 split in detail — three Pentagon fears

Each of these gets its own full three-act video with its own cold open, its own Act 2 explanation, and its own God paragraph. The three share the Part 2 slot (numbered 2A, 2B, 2C) because they all live inside the same MadHoney chapter and the same general "Pentagon" audience.

### Part 2A — DARPA Onion-Peeler

**The fear:** Industrialized parallel social engineering against every DARPA employee and every member of their family simultaneously. A RajaBee coordinates GiantQueens per DARPA office, each GiantQueen assigns DwarfQueens to individual employees, each DwarfQueen's Workers target the employee's wife, children, father, divorce lawyer, personal trainer, former college roommate. Each Worker runs a small local model. Every family member is probed at once. The system does not sleep. It does not get bored. Every person is a layer of the onion. Klaus Fuchs walked out of the Manhattan Project with the entire atomic bomb design because the Soviets got lucky. With this system, you do not need luck. You probe every employee simultaneously until you find your Fuchs, and you can have ten of them, across different departments, none of them knowing about each other.

**The secretary cold open:** A retired DARPA engineer's wife receives a sweet-looking recipe blog link from "a fellow mom in the school WhatsApp." She clicks. Every photo on his phone, every contact in his address book, every classified conversation he ever had at his own kitchen table is now in the hands of a foreign intelligence service. No break-in. No hacker in a hoodie. A recipe.

**The God paragraph (DARPA director / senior program manager):** Your technical security is irrelevant. Your encrypted networks are irrelevant. Your air-gapped classified systems are irrelevant. The chain is only as strong as its weakest link, and the weakest link is the spouse of every engineer on your payroll, scrolling social media in her kitchen. The Iranian Handala group broke Israel's top five security officials through personal Gmail and Telegram with human hackers taking months per target. Our system does every DARPA employee, and every DARPA employee's family, in parallel, forever.

### Part 2B — Pentagon Matrix / The Hierarchical Lie Machine

**The fear:** A distributed AI hive generates a complete false reality across every information channel the Pentagon uses — satellite feeds, radar returns, signals intelligence, social media, encrypted phone lines — all consistent with each other, all in real time, all pointing at the same fake war. The Pentagon's cross-referencing immune system is designed to catch one faked source by comparing it to the others; the hive fakes all of the sources at once. Doctor Strangelove's rogue general was a cartoon because only one man had to be deceived; the hive delivers a personally tailored false reality to exactly the one general with launch authority. Mutually Assured Destruction assumes both sides want to survive; it does not work on Iran's Revolutionary Guard, North Korea, or ISIS-style actors with bunkers. They are not trying to survive. They are trying to trick two superpowers into destroying each other while they wait underground.

**The secretary cold open:** A senior American general authorized a catastrophic real-world action this morning — an action no sane commander would ever authorize — based on briefings, satellite images, and a video call with the Secretary of Defense that were all generated over the preceding six months by a distributed AI specifically designed to break his judgment. He did not know. His own aides did not know. His own screens lied to him. The action is now irreversible. The anchor cannot say what the action was. She can say what the result was. The result is on the screen behind her.

**The God paragraph (Pentagon decision-maker):** You do not need to hack the President. You need to hack one general with launch authority. One submarine commander. One silo colonel. Generate a personalized false reality just for him — his screens, his phone line, the voice of his superiors on the encrypted channel — and he will turn the key because everything he trusts is telling him to. The Prisoner's Dilemma with nuclear weapons does not require both superpowers to agree to strike. It requires exactly one deceived officer on one platform to launch one missile. One missile is sufficient. The machine that creates the deception does not need to persuade a country. It needs to persuade one man.

### Part 2C — DoD Maginot Bullseye

**The fear:** Every American military AI data center is a known physical location, filled with thousands of GPUs that consume megawatts of power and require industrial cooling. One $50,000 cruise missile disables a $2 billion AI center. One compromised janitor with a USB stick does the same. Cut the power substation, cut the fiber, detonate an EMP above the continental US, and the entire centralized American military AI stack is dark for months — while the distributed Chinese hive, running on millions of civilian computers across 1.4 billion people, loses a fraction of a percent of its capacity and keeps going. The DoD is building the Maginot Line of AI. The enemy does not attack it head-on. The enemy walks around it.

**The secretary cold open:** An American military AI data center in Texas — the one that coordinates the drones, guides the weapons, and analyzes the intelligence — goes dark at 3:47 AM. A single cruise missile destroyed the cooling system. The GPUs burned themselves out in eleven minutes. American military AI capability is offline across two theaters. The anchor cannot say which theaters. She can say the replacement timeline. The replacement timeline is eighteen months.

**The God paragraph (DoD procurement / senior uniformed leadership):** You spent $886 billion and built an AI capability that can be destroyed by a single cruise missile, sabotaged by a single insider, disabled by cutting one substation, and fried by one high-altitude EMP. China built the same capability across millions of civilian computers, in millions of homes, in millions of locations. In an exchange of blows, you lose everything in minutes and they lose a fraction of a percent. Ukraine already proved this on February 24, 2022 — the centralized systems died on day one and the distributed improvised systems survived the war. The DoD is building the approach that Ukraine lost. The Chinese hive is the approach that Ukraine won with.

## Chapter 4 split in detail — three pharma bundles

Pharma is different from Pentagon. Pharma has ten distinct angles in the chapter, but the angel-forwardable secretary beats and the manager-terrifying boardroom beats do not map one-to-one. So the right structure is **bundled pairs**: each video pairs one secretary fear with one DIFFERENT manager fear. Three bundles total. The pairs are chosen by visceral strength, not by topic alignment, and the link between the two halves of each pair is there when it is natural and forced when it is not.

**How the bundling works in the 3-act structure of each video:**
- **Act 1 Cold Open (0:00–0:30):** the secretary beat, delivered by the anchor `1 MONTH FROM NOW` style.
- **Act 2 first half (0:30–1:00):** flashback, the word "swarm," concrete real-life consequences of the secretary beat.
- **Act 2 second half (1:00–1:15):** the God paragraph — a different fear, aimed at the manager, thematically linked where possible.
- **Act 3 (1:15–end):** standard ending card — Strulovitz / slogan / Part N/14 / link to MadHoney.

### Part 8A — "Ozempic at $14"

**Secretary (cold open):** She has been paying $1,000 a month for a pill that costs $14 in Mumbai. Her husband has been paying the same for his. Her sister has been paying for the weight-loss version for two years. The molecule is the same molecule. Forty-plus Indian generic manufacturers have been selling it since the Indian patent expired on March 20, 2026. She just found out. She is not angry. She is calculating how much of her retirement has been eaten by one pill.

**Manager (God paragraph):** China already manufactures your GLP-1 franchise. WuXi AppTec currently makes twenty-four GLP-1 drugs across clinical and commercial stages, and grew its peptide manufacturing business ninety-six percent year over year on the back of your demand. Hengrui is already filing patents on the next-generation GLP-1 derivatives, in Shanghai, and extending them to sixty countries through the Patent Cooperation Treaty before your own patent office has heard of them. BeiGene has rebranded as BeOne Medicines and redomiciled to Switzerland specifically to continue beating you under a friendlier passport. The trillion-dollar future of Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly — the future priced into your 2026 guidance, the future your shareholders are counting on, the future your executive bonuses are structured around — is already being manufactured, patented, and rebranded by people who are not you. Your moat was consumer ignorance. The local model destroys the ignorance in four seconds in every American kitchen simultaneously.

**The link:** She learns the pill is $14. You learn the Chinese make it and already own the next one. Same topic, two sides of the same mirror.

### Part 8B — "The Cascade Killed Grandpa"

**Secretary (cold open):** Her grandfather started on one statin at age 55. He felt fine. He was running twice a week. By 57 the muscle pain started, and his doctor told him the labs looked normal so the pain must be imagined. By 61 he was diabetic from the statin and on metformin. By 62 he was on a proton pump inhibitor for the stomach trouble from the metformin, and nobody reassessed it, so he stayed on it for six years. By 68 he was confused and forgetful, and the neurologist diagnosed mild cognitive impairment. At 67 he broke his hip getting out of the shower because the acid-reflux pill had been quietly stripping calcium from his bones for twelve years. He was given opioids for the post-surgical pain. He was in the unlucky one percent. At 69 he died — demented, diabetic, surgically repaired, and addicted to painkillers prescribed for a hip broken by a pill prescribed for a stomach problem caused by a pill prescribed for a disease caused by the first pill. Every prescription was legal. Every prescription was in the manufacturer's own peer-reviewed literature. Nobody had ever drawn the arrows between them. She just saw the arrows on her local model in four seconds.

**Manager (God paragraph):** The ad channel you bet your 2026 revenue guidance on — the future advertising channel that was going to replace television, replace Google, replace the pharmaceutical sales rep, replace the doctor dinner, and feed your industry for the next twenty years — dies the day patients start asking a local model instead of ChatGPT. OpenAI has already told you, in public, that its advertising system will "not appear near sensitive topics, including health." That sentence is the confession. The moment that lock comes off, or the moment enough patients route around it by using a local hive, your paid-placement revenue evaporates. The sixteen-billion-dollar AI-in-pharma market projected for 2034 — every deal your industry has signed with OpenAI, Anthropic, Novartis-Isomorphic, Lilly-Isomorphic, Novo-NVIDIA — becomes a stranded asset. You did not buy drug discovery. You bought a seat at a table that is about to be set on fire by patients answering their own questions for free.

**The link:** Grandpa's prescription chain was your old recurring revenue. ChatGPT ads were going to be your new one. The local model breaks both — the arrows and the advertisements — in the same kitchen, in the same four seconds.

### Part 8C — "McKinsey Priced Him at $5,465"

**Secretary (cold open):** Her brother is dead from a pill a doctor prescribed him. The pill was OxyContin. The doctor was told by Purdue Pharma's sales representatives that the risk of addiction was "less than one percent" — a phrase Purdue knew was a lie when they wrote it into their sales training materials. Six hundred and forty-five thousand Americans died from opioids between 1999 and 2021, including her brother. Purdue hired McKinsey to help them sell more OxyContin. McKinsey proposed in a written document that Purdue pay pharmacies fourteen thousand eight hundred and ten dollars for every customer who overdosed or became addicted — priced per body, on paper, in a formal client deliverable. When the lawsuits came, the Sackler family withdrew ten billion dollars from Purdue before the courts could reach it. McKinsey eventually settled the whole affair for approximately five thousand four hundred and sixty-five dollars per dead American — less than their own per-body proposal. McKinsey made a profit on the settlement. The consulting firm that priced her brother's death got a refund on it. Less than a used car.

**Manager (God paragraph):** Right now your insurer pays Palantir to quietly flag which of her relatives costs too much to keep alive. When hospitals start running AI on their own computers, that data stops leaving the building — and Palantir goes dark. The insurer loses its kill list. And every friend you have in the insurance business loses money the same week you do, because you were all making money off the same sick patients.

**The link:** McKinsey priced her brother. Palantir prices her sister. Same coalition. Same game. Both rent-extraction machines run on data they were not supposed to be allowed to see. The local model on her kitchen table sees more than any McKinsey memo ever did, and it tells her for free.

## What we dropped from the pharma chapter

Seven of the ten angles in Chapter 4 do not become videos. They remain in the book for the board, not in the video series for the angels:

- **Angle 4 — AlphaFold killer / drug discovery without Google's permission.** Boardroom beat about $3B Isomorphic-Lilly-Novartis deals. No angel pull.
- **Angle 5 — Monoculture and Confusion Industry.** Nutrition honesty argument. Too abstract for a 90-second video. Fits in the book.
- **Angle 6A — Single-drug hidden damage.** Statin/PPI/SSRI risks. Rolled into 8B's cascade cold open instead of getting its own video.
- **Angle 6D — CIMAvax / Cuba.** The 15-year Cuban lung cancer vaccine. Powerful but niche — the audience that cares already knows, the audience that doesn't will not learn it in 90 seconds.
- **Angle 6E — Pusztai / GMO / food allergies.** The Bt-toxin-to-leaky-gut-to-allergy chain. Too speculative and too explanatory for the angel format. Stays in the book.
- **Angle 7 — Sales rep replacement.** Doctor-gets-whispered-to-at-8:47pm. Great mechanism, but the angel has no hook — her doctor does not appear in her terror vocabulary.
- **Angle 9 — Counterfeit apocalypse.** Visceral (kid's pill from Walgreens identical to cartel pill) but conceptually confusing in 30 seconds. Needs more setup than the cold open allows.
- **Angle 10 — FDA is only the front door.** Meta-conclusion that closes the chapter. Not a standalone video — the whole point is that it summarizes the other nine windows.

These are not failures of the chapter. They are failures of the 90-second-angel format, which is narrower than the book format by design. The book is for the board. The video is for the secretary. The board can read ten angles. The secretary watches three.

## Numbering impact on the bible's completionist mechanic

The `/10` mechanic in the original bible exists because a numbered finite set creates completionist binge behavior. The expansion to `/14` preserves the mechanic — the set is still finite, and fourteen is still a small enough number that a committed viewer will watch the whole series — but the specific "ten" count is gone.

If the `/10` has already been burned into titles, thumbnails, or end-cards, update all of them to `/14`. The format stays the same:

> **`How China will beat America - <Industry> (Part N/14)`**

Examples for the full new slate:

- `How China will beat America - Drones (Part 1/14)`
- `How China will beat America - DARPA (Part 2A/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Pentagon (Part 2B/14)`
- `How China will beat America - DoD (Part 2C/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Humanoids (Part 3/14)`

- `How China will beat America - Defense Contractors (Part 5/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Big Tech (Part 6/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Wall Street (Part 7/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Ozempic at $14 (Part 8A/14)`
- `How China will beat America - The Cascade Killed Grandpa (Part 8B/14)`
- `How China will beat America - McKinsey Priced Him at $5,465 (Part 8C/14)`
- `How China will beat America - Europe (Part 9/14)`

The 2A/2B/2C and 8A/8B/8C sub-numbering keeps the Pentagon and Pharma parts visually grouped for the binge-watcher without inflating the headline number past 14.
