# BeeSting Episode 3 — Block 1 (Opening) Narration
# Total: ~56 seconds
# Pacing: equation slide ~6-7 sec, each nightmare ~10 sec
# Voice: Samantha (ElevenLabs)
# Tone: cold transactional, monkey-wrench — NOT moral outrage. The "you" is the Big Pharma executive watching this.


[EQUATION SLIDE — ~6-7 sec]
The revolutionary Israeli software, giving every American patient a free Chinese doctor in her kitchen, will collapse Big Pharma tomorrow. Indianapolis is about to become the new Detroit.


[NIGHTMARE A — Medicine Cabinet Avalanche — 10 sec]
Your patient's medicine cabinet. Every bottle inside is your revenue. In her kitchen, a free Chinese doctor now tells her what each pill is doing to her. She stops swallowing.


[NIGHTMARE B — Family Dinner Subtraction — 10 sec]
Her family dinner. One chair empties from the painkiller you sold her father. Another from the antidepressant her brother was prescribed. Another from her mother's heartburn pill. The dinner stops paying you.


[NIGHTMARE C — Insurance Letter — 10 sec]
The denial letter your insurance partner just mailed her. She walks away from your pharmacy. She buys the same drug from Mumbai for the price of a coffee.


[NIGHTMARE D — Body Price Tag — 10 sec]
Her body, the way McKinsey priced it on a slide for you. Liver. Hip. Memory. Overdose. Every part a billing code. The codes are walking out of your balance sheet.


[NIGHTMARE E — American Cemetery — 10 sec]
The American graveyard. You filled it, prescription by prescription, with more dead Americans than every war since Vietnam. The next stones carry your shareholders' names.
