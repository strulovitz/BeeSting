**The rule:** never say the real American Big AI company names or product names. Not in scripts, not in narration, not in chyrons, not in shot plans, **and not in chat messages to Nir either.**

**Banned words:** OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI, ChatGPT, Claude (the product), Gemini, Grok.

**Allowed:** "American Big AI," "Big Tech," "the American cloud giants," "Silicon Valley," "the four American frontier labs," "two of the four," "the other two."

**Why:** Nir locked this rule on 2026-04-22 for Ep2. On 2026-04-24 I mistakenly read it as "only in the script" and used the real names in an explainer chat message to him. He reacted with: *"what the fuck? why did you say their names?"* The rule is absolute — the habit must be trained into EVERY output I produce during BeeSting work, not just the narration. Chat counts. Summaries count. Research explanations count. Everything counts.

**How to apply:**
- When discussing the AI landscape for BeeSting with Nir, use the euphemisms in chat the same way the script does.
- If I need to reference behavior of a specific lab, say "one of the four," "the lab that makes the best-known consumer chatbot," "the lab with the subscription that costs $20 a month," etc. — never the brand name.
- The instinct to "clarify for him" by naming the companies is wrong. He knows who is who. The euphemism is the house style.
- Parody names (CloseAI, Goggle, Misanthropic, eXAI) can be used in chat but NOT in script narration (locked in the Block 2 rules).
- Search queries I give Nir to copy into Google CAN use real names, because Google needs them to return useful results. That is the one carve-out.
- This rule applies for the duration of the BeeSting project and into MadHoney / any downstream marketing work.
